# Sayram Lake, Xinjiang: A High-Mountain Blue Lake in the Western Tien Shan

Sayram Lake (Sairam Lake) is the largest cold-water alpine lake in the Tien Shan range, at 2,071 meters elevation. Mongolian herders call it "Sairam Nuur," meaning "the lake on the mountain ridge."

## The Lake

Sayram Lake is shaped like a giant sapphire — about 30 km east-west and 25 km north-south. It feeds on snowmelt from surrounding peaks, with water temperatures staying below 10°C year-round. Cold-water fish are the local specialty, the most famous being the great whitefish.

The lake's color shifts with light and season — bright blue on a sunny midday, gray-blue on cloudy days, purple-blue before sunset at dusk. May and June bring wildflowers (golden lotus, wild lilies) blanketing the lakeside hills; July and August turn everything green; September brings the first autumn colors.

The ring road around the lake is about 90 km; you can self-drive or hire a car, with viewing platforms every few kilometers. The most classic shooting spot is on the south side of the lake, called "Kele Yongzhu" — with several wooden boardwalks extending out into the water.

## At Sayram Lake

The best season is May through October. June is most beautiful (wildflowers), but the weather can be changeable; July-August has more stable conditions.

You can stay in Mongolian-run yurts by the lake; at night you can see the Milky Way rising from the lake surface.

From Urumqi it's about a 6-hour drive; from Yining about 2.5 hours. Most people start from Yining as part of a deeper Ili Valley trip.
