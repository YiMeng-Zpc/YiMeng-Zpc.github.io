# Ili, Xinjiang: Nalati and Gongnaisi Grasslands with Snow Mountains

The Ili River valley is home to some of the best high-mountain grassland scenery in western China. Nalati and Gongnaisi are the two most famous alpine meadows.

## Nalati

"Nalati" means "the place where the sun rises" in Mongolian. At about 1,800 meters elevation, it's a classic high-mountain valley grassland — meadows stretching from your feet to the distant Tien Shan snowline. Wildflowers (golden lotus, wild tulips) cover everything in June, deepest green in July-August, turning gold in September.

The scenic area is divided into Air Grassland, River Valley Grassland, and Snow Spruce Forest. The Air Grassland needs a 30-minute shuttle bus ride to the top. Once there you can ride horses or hike. The snow spruce is endemic to western China — straight trunks, can grow over 60 meters tall.

## Gongnaisi

Gongnaisi is north of Nalati, more remote and less visited. The Gongnaisi Forest Farm is one of China's four great forest farms — spruce forest lines both sides of the road. Wildflowers in spring and summer, golden birch in autumn, plus Kazakh herders and their sheep grazing on the meadows — the whole scene like an oil painting.

Gongnaisi's other highlight is Banchan Gou — a small temple in the valley where a Panchen Lama once resided. A 2-hour hike takes you to the untouched original spruce forest.

## In Ili

The best season is May to October. Summer is most pleasant but also the most crowded; late September autumn colors are the most beautiful.

Stay in Xinyuan County or Nalati Town. Local Kazakh-run yurts offer accommodation; the hosts cook hand-pulled lamb and milk tea, giving you a real high-mountain grassland experience.
