# Ili, Xinjiang: Nalati and Gongnaisi Grasslands with Snow Mountains

The Ili River valley is home to some of the best high-mountain grassland scenery in western China. Nalati and Gongnaisi are the two most famous alpine meadows.

## Nalati

"Nalati" means "the place where the sun rises" in Mongolian. At about 1,800 meters elevation, it's a classic high-mountain valley grassland.

This is the thaw, not summer. The meadow has already turned green — cleanly green — but the snow hasn't finished leaving: white patches lie across the slopes, gathered in hollows and under tree shadows; the ridge in the distance still carries a line of old snow pressed low under the cloud. The spruce forest is still loaded with it, white to the tips. Sunlight leaks through gaps in the cloud and splits the slope into two greens — yellow-green where the light lands, almost black where it doesn't.

There are wildflowers, but nothing like the travel posters promise: small yellow blooms, the dandelion kind, crowding into the gaps between river stones; the wide-open meadow itself is empty.

The scenic area is divided into Air Grassland, River Valley Grassland, and Snow Spruce Forest. The Air Grassland needs a 30-minute shuttle bus ride to the top. Once there you can ride horses or hike. The snow spruce is endemic to western China — straight trunks, can grow over 60 meters tall. Those ranks of dark green spires standing along the slope are exactly that: loaded with snow, and taller than anything around them.

## Gongnaisi

Gongnaisi is north of Nalati, more remote and less visited. The Gongnaisi Forest Farm is one of China's four great forest farms — spruce forest lines both sides of the road.

Gongnaisi looks like this: a line of snow spruce marching across a gentle slope, dead branches bowed down under the snow; further out, a ridge thick with dark green scrub, its skyline rolling low and easy; beyond that, the snow mountains. The grass holds that early-spring mix of yellow-green and tender green, and the birches and other deciduous trees are still bare — not one of them has turned gold.

Not a single person appears on the meadows, only wind.

Gongnaisi's other highlight is Banchan Gou — a small temple in the valley where a Panchen Lama once resided. A 2-hour hike takes you to the untouched original spruce forest.

## In Ili

The best season is May to October. Summer is most pleasant but also the most crowded; late September autumn colors are the most beautiful. But the thaw suggests a different reason to come: April, in the middle of it. The grass has just turned, the snow hasn't gone, the spruce is loaded — and for a few minutes the contrast between them is stronger than anything summer has.

Stay in Xinyuan County or Nalati Town. Local Kazakh-run yurts offer accommodation; the hosts cook hand-pulled lamb and milk tea, giving you a real high-mountain grassland experience.
